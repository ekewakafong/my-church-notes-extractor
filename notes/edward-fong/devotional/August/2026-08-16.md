# Fret Not, Trust in the Lord

## Morning 🌄

### What Does “Fret” Mean in the Scripture Context?

The Hebrew word חרה (*charah*) can describe anger being kindled or becoming hot. In Psalm 37, “fret” includes:

- Being angry and agitated
- Being resentful or envious
- Being emotionally consumed by the apparent success of evildoers

### Psalm 37:1–2

So don't be heated or angry because of evildoers / workers of iniquity, for they shall soon be cut down like the grass and wither as the green herb.

> “They shall soon be cut down like the grass, and wither as the green herb.”
[Psalm 37:1–2](https://bible.com/bible/114/PSA.37.1-2.NKJV)

### Psalm 37:3–4

> “Trust in the Lord, and do good; Dwell in the land and feed on His faithfulness. Delight yourself also in the Lord, And He shall give you the desires of your heart.”
[Psalm 37:3–4](https://bible.com/bible/114/PSA.37.3-4.NKJV)

### Psalm 37:8

Cease from anger and forsake wrath. Do not fret—it only causes harm.

> “Cease from anger, and forsake wrath; Do not fret—it only causes harm.”
[Psalm 37:8](https://bible.com/bible/114/PSA.37.8.NKJV)

### Revelation 1:3

There is blessing for those who read, hear, and keep the words of the prophecy.

> “Blessed is he who reads and those who hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written in it.”
[Revelation 1:3](https://bible.com/bible/114/REV.1.3.NKJV)

### Revelation 1:6

God has made us kings and priests to His God and Father.

> “And has made us kings and priests to His God and Father.”
[Revelation 1:6](https://bible.com/bible/114/REV.1.6.NKJV)

## Summary

- “Fret” involves becoming heated, angry, agitated, resentful, or envious.
- Don't become angry because of evildoers or be consumed by their apparent success.
- Trust in the Lord, do good, and feed on His faithfulness.
- Delight yourself in the Lord and cease from anger and wrath.
- Do not fret, because it only causes harm.
